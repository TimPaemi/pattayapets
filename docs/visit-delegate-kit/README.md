# Visit evidence kit — PattayaPets

Internal workflow for a human first-hand visit. A visit is never implied by a source check,
phone call, map listing or AI-generated draft. No completed visit record is currently
published.

## Publication boundary

1. The operator selects a business whose operating and publication state is already approved.
2. A human visits as an ordinary customer, pays the full public price and keeps private proof.
3. The human completes `post-visit-template.md` after leaving. Clinical details, staff/private
   names and unrelated personal data stay out of the publishable section.
4. A second human checks the receipt/date, conflict declaration, scope and wording.
5. Only the operator can approve a public visit date, byline, evidence scope and verdict.
6. The source record is updated, the business/data gates run, and release remains a separate
   operator action. AI may draft wording; it may not approve a verdict, invent evidence, commit,
   deploy or request indexing.

Use the mailbox currently configured in `src/site-config.js` only after the operator has tested
delivery. Do not put private visit evidence in the public repository or generated site.

## Non-negotiable rules

- No PR-arranged, hosted, discounted or disclosed review visit.
- The reviewer pays the ordinary bill and records any unexpected discount.
- A verdict is limited to the observable customer experience: access, communication, quoted
  versus billed price, cleanliness and handling that a customer can directly observe.
- Never assess diagnosis, treatment choice, medication, surgery, clinical outcome, licensing or
  regulatory compliance without the appropriate qualified evidence.
- One visit is one dated observation, not a permanent claim about every shift or future customer.
- No score, star rating, `Review` schema or `AggregateRating` is generated from this kit.
- Conflicts, prior relationships and gifts are disclosed internally before approval.
- The business may correct facts but cannot buy, preview, suppress or negotiate a verdict.

## Files

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `tier-1-briefs.md` | A pre-visit checklist generated from current approved data, not a stale contact list. |
| `post-visit-template.md` | Private evidence and a separately marked publishable observation draft. |

The authoritative business state remains the approved public model and its dossier. If this kit
and those records disagree, stop and reconcile the evidence before arranging a visit.
