# PATTAYA PETS FULL AUDIT — 2026-07-28

## 0. How to read this

This is a read-only audit. No source, generated page, index directive, build artifact, deployment, cache, or third-party account was changed.

Severity means:

- **S1 — act now:** actively harmful to traffic, indexing, trust, safety, or regulated accuracy.
- **S2 — fix next:** blocks a meaningful upside or leaves a material avoidable risk.
- **S3 — clean-up:** dead weight, inconsistency, or maintainability debt.
- **S4 — note:** observation with no urgent action.

The command IDs in brackets point to the exact reproducible commands in §12. Counts are from those commands. Manual editorial judgements are labelled as such. Primary regulatory and search sources cited below were reopened on 2026-07-28.

Scope checked:

The local `main` ref is **3 commits ahead** of `origin/main`; the audited local commit is `e6402cf` [C01]. Generated `dist/` is ignored. This report therefore describes the current local tree plus the live site where explicitly stated, not merely the last pushed revision.

## 1. Is anything wrong?

**Yes. The site is technically healthy, but six S1 issues need action before the next ordinary content/deploy cycle.**

The good news is substantial: the build gate passes, internal links are clean, the live sitemap is fully reachable, canonical redirects are one-hop and consistent, pages are small, mobile overflow checks pass, and no exposed secret was found.

The urgent problems are more human than mechanical:

1. export pages tell readers U-Tapao may be usable even though the current DLD animal-quarantine-station list does not name it;
2. the South Korea guides overstate routine advance-permission requirements and point at the wrong authority domain;
3. an emergency-adjacent mobile-vet listing has no contact route;
4. the footer claims in-person checking across every page without repo evidence that supports that blanket statement;
5. the corrections page says no corrections exist even though the audited history names substantive corrections;
6. the documented/default deploy path can bypass the new guarded deploy script, recreating the class of error that previously published the wrong site.

These are narrow, repairable faults. They do not justify a broad rewrite, a framework migration, or bulk-generated content.

## 3. S1 — act now

### S1.1 Remove the unsupported U-Tapao export route

- **What:** **29** built export pages say that U-Tapao may sometimes work, and/or instruct the reader to apply to the animal quarantine station at the actual departure airport. The current official DLD station list names Suvarnabhumi, Don Mueang, Phuket, Chiang Mai, and other stations, but not U-Tapao. This is regulated travel advice, not harmless route inspiration.
- **Where:** shared text at `src/data/richness-blocks.js:20-31` and `src/data/richness-blocks.js:152-153`; export rendering at `src/pages/41-export.js:246-247` and `src/pages/41-export.js:365-366`; destination variants including `src/pages/59-export-seo-countries.js:141-142` and `src/pages/59-export-seo-countries.js:155-156`. The affected outputs are the three core export guides plus all **26** `to-*` destination guides [C12].
- **Evidence/count:** the targeted false-claim scan returns **29** unique generated pages [C12].
- **Why now:** DLD’s current official animal-quarantine-station list was reopened on 2026-07-28: [Department of Livestock Development — animal quarantine stations](https://aqi.dld.go.th/webnew/images/stories/document/data-import-export/transit-date_eng.pdf). Commit `97cc733` corrected the import-side U-Tapao claim but left the export-side variants in place [C01].
- **Severity:** **S1** — a reader could plan a regulated departure around a station the responsible authority does not list.
- **Smallest safe fix, not applied:** remove every U-Tapao export-permission/AQS claim and tell readers to use Suvarnabhumi unless a current DLD primary source explicitly names U-Tapao. Do not replace it with another uncited airport guess.

### S1.2 Rewrite the South Korea requirements against the current Korean checklist

- **What:** the Korea guides present advance permission, an import licence, and a quarantine reservation as normal requirements. The current Korean government guidance instead centers on the government export quarantine certificate, ISO-compatible microchip, rabies-antibody result where applicable, declaration, and inspection. The cited embassy guidance limits advance notification/prior facility approval to shipments of **10 or more** cats or dogs.
- **Where:** `src/pages/59-export-seo-countries.js:169-215` and `src/pages/58-import-seo-countries.js:227-231`; outputs `/take-pet-out-of-thailand/to-south-korea.html` and `/bring-pet-to-thailand/from-south-korea.html`. `animal.go.kr` is also labelled as APQA even though the responsible quarantine authority’s domain is `qia.go.kr`.
- **Evidence/count:** **2** built country guides are affected [C22].
- **Why now:** [Republic of Korea MOFA pet-entry guidance](https://overseas.mofa.go.kr/no-en/brd/m_25180/view.do?seq=9), published 2023-12-11, and the current [Incheon Airport animal-quarantine procedure](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1443/subview.do) and [declaration guidance](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1479/subview.do) were reopened on 2026-07-28.
- **Severity:** **S1** — the site overstates regulated prerequisites and lead time while mislabelling the source authority.
- **Smallest safe fix, not applied:** replace the permission/licence/reservation sequence with the current certificate–microchip–titre–inspection checklist; state the **10+ animal** exception precisely; replace the source link with the relevant QIA/MOFA/Incheon primary page.

### The IndexNow false success

This is §9 item 10 in the deploy path rather than the link auditor: a 4xx is a refusal, not a
submission. Because `npm run deploy` ends with `&& npm run indexnow`, every URL could be silently
rejected on every deploy and the output would still read as success.

`scripts/ping-indexnow.js` now:

- treats only **200/202** as accepted and counts refusals separately;
- prints `ok`/`FAIL` per request instead of a bare status line;
