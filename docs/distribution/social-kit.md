# Social distribution kit — PattayaPets

This is an operator template, not a claim that any page is ready to promote. Before using a post, open the landing page, check its visible reviewed date and sources, and confirm that any business named on it is approved for publication. Regulated travel and emergency pages must also pass the current claim and clinical-review gates.

Use the canonical URL emitted by the current build. Do not copy a URL from this file after a canonical migration. Organic posts should use a plain URL; paid campaigns should use the approved campaign convention in `ads-map.md`.

Contact details must come from `src/site-config.js`. Do not paste a phone, LINE, WhatsApp, or mailbox from an old document.

## Publication checklist

- The landing URL is the current canonical and returns a direct success response.
- The visible reviewed date matches metadata and structured data.
- Regulated or medical claims have the required approval state.
- Every named business is publishable; held records and restricted contacts are absent.
- The post makes no price, duration, outcome, superlative, availability, or endorsement claim that the page cannot prove.
- The post is useful in the target community and complies with that community’s rules.
- No cross-project brand or publication, private contact, affiliate link, paid-placement claim, or unapproved tracking value appears.
- Corrections can be sent through the contact path shown on the live page.

## Cadence

Publish for a real reader need, not to satisfy a fixed posting quota. Avoid repeating substantially identical wording in the same community. Record the date, destination, landing URL, and final copy so inaccurate posts can be corrected or withdrawn when a source changes.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- No automated posting from generated page metadata.
- No “complete,” “definitive,” “guaranteed,” “official,” or “2027” claim unless the page itself proves the relevant scope.
- No emergency-treatment excerpts without clinical approval.
- No promotion of a regulated page while its claim ledger is expired, incomplete, or on hold.
- No promotion of a listing whose operating status, locality, service scope, or contact publication basis is unresolved.
